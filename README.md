# Uchaaran Helper

An ustaad records how each word and pankti should be said. Ustaad not only speaks in the microphone to record the sample, but also writes in his own words in english on pronunciation rules. The child says it back, and the AI scores how close they came and points to the exact word to practise again. The ustaad sets the standard; the AI only measures against it.

## Follow along, word by word

As the paath plays, the child taps each word as it is recited. A word tapped in time lights up; a missed or wrong word gets a red underline. The audio and text must stay aligned word by word, in real time.

## Read aloud, with the app listening.

The child recites on their own, and the app follows their voice through the bani, highlighting where they are and marking words to revisit.

## Progress that pulls them back

Streaks, banis completed and words mastered, with a short summary for parents. Every reward follows attention to Gurbani, never time spent in the app.

## Parents in control

Kids Mode is switched on by a parent in Settings, and a child's recordings stay on the device unless a parent chooses otherwise.

## Tested before trusted

The Uchaaran Helper's scores are checked against an ustaad's own judgement of the same recordings, and the follow-along against word timings marked by hand.
