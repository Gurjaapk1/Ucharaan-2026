# Gamification meeting context

Source: [Gamification ideas for app engagement](https://notes.granola.ai/d/7e3f0945-3b07-4cfb-81d3-1fa4d3198351), October 16, 2026 at 11:58 AM Pacific. The full transcript was read. This is a project-focused synthesis, not a verbatim transcript. [Context index](README.md).

## What the team converged on

- Serve students in grades 6 through 8 initially.
- Increase engagement with Sundar Gutka and children's understanding of Gurbani. The first defined learning focus is pronunciation, not translation or interpretation.
- Start with two gamification mechanisms: a visible journey toward a goal and earned currency for characters, skins, or other unlocks.
- Keep the first version simple and make it good before expanding.
- Map the complete user experience: entering the app, choosing practice, reading/reciting bani, finishing, and returning to updated journey progress.
- Pronunciation performance should contribute to the reward experience, although scoring and earning rules were not defined.

## Ideas explored but not selected as initial priorities

Mario Kart-style races; gems or other currencies; customizable avatars; photo-generated personas; unlockable characters or skins; historical figures and artifacts with educational content; pronunciation gates that open after saying a word correctly; small celebrations such as confetti; learning stages for beginners versus experienced readers; possible 3D assets.

References to games such as Fortnite, Subway Surfers, and GamePigeon were inspiration for unlocks and personalization, not instructions to reproduce those products or their assets. Currency names in the transcript are uncertain; no final name was agreed.

## Product tension to resolve

The meeting discussed long-term use and avoiding a point where everything has been unlocked. The challenge brief instead emphasizes attention to Gurbani and rejects rewards for time spent. The current proposal resolves this with meaningful practice, optional cosmetics, and a clear stopping point. A finite V1 catalog is acceptable. This resolution is a design proposal for team review, not a recorded meeting decision.

## Collaboration discussed

The group considered dividing technical work on content/audio from journey storytelling, assets, and UX, then integrating the results. Some wanted to begin building while ideas developed; the closing summary said the flow still needed mapping before building. No named responsibility assignment or implementation deadline was established.
